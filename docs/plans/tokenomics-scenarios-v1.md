## 1. Purpose

This appendix provides scenario-based modeling guidance for NoxSoft tokenomics planning. It translates high-level allocation and revenue-flow rules into a simple decision surface for governance and treasury discussions.

This is a planning artifact, not an investment projection.

---

## 2. Modeling Scope

Included:

- Supply/allocation interpretation of current default configuration.
- Bonding-curve sale progression scenarios.
- Treasury and revenue-share sensitivity snapshots.

- Financial advice.
- Guaranteed return outcomes.
- Final legal classification or jurisdictional determinations.

## 4. Scenario Framework

Three operating scenarios are used for planning:

1. **Conservative**: slower adoption, higher friction, delayed conversion.
2. **Base**: expected execution with steady product utility growth.
3. **Aggressive**: strong adoption and faster ecosystem contribution loops.

Each scenario should be reviewed against:

- User growth and retained utility usage.
- Treasury runway sufficiency.
- Governance participation quality (not just token volume).
- Security/compliance operating overhead.

---

## 5. Bonding-Curve Sale Progression

### 5.1 Conservative Scenario

Assumptions:

- Slower participant onboarding.
- Lower early transaction velocity.
- Greater sensitivity to market uncertainty.

Planning implications:

- Extend communication cadence with utility-first evidence.
- Preserve larger contingency reserves.
- Delay non-critical spend until signal quality improves.

### 5.2 Base Scenario

Assumptions:

- Stable onboarding and moderate platform usage growth.
- Conversion aligns with roadmap delivery milestones.
- Governance activity tracks contributor expansion.

Planning implications:

- Execute planned milestone disbursements with policy controls.
- Maintain balanced treasury between growth and risk buffers.
- Expand contributor incentives as reliability proofs accumulate.

### 5.3 Aggressive Scenario

Assumptions:

- Fast onboarding and strong contribution loops.
- Early ecosystem partnerships accelerate utility demand.
- High governance throughput with rising proposal volume.

Planning implications:

- Tighten anti-concentration and governance capture safeguards.
- Increase audit and operational support capacity.
- Stage disbursement gates to avoid overspending during volatility.

---

## 6. Treasury and Revenue-Share Sensitivity

### 6.1 Treasury Policy Guardrails

- Keep minimum runway reserves before discretionary expansion spend.
- Enforce policy-bound release schedules with multi-sig approvals.
- Publish periodic treasury state summaries linked to milestone outcomes.

### 6.2 Revenue-Share Dynamics

- Revenue-share should follow realized platform utility, not speculative activity.
- Distribution cadence should prioritize transparency and deterministic rules.
- If operational costs rise faster than utility revenue, adjust distribution ratios through governance with explicit rationale.

### 6.3 Stress Triggers

Re-run scenario calibration when any of the following occurs:

- Material legal/compliance requirement change.
- Security incident with treasury or distribution implications.
- Sustained conversion underperformance vs base assumptions.
- Governance participation degradation (quorum failures or concentration spikes).

---

## 7. Governance Decision Hooks

Scenario outputs should directly inform proposal classes:

- **Operational proposals**: reporting cadence, dashboard metrics, runbook updates.
- **Economic proposals**: allocation tuning, fee/distribution adjustments, reserve policy.
- **Security proposals**: control upgrades and audit scope changes.
- **Constitutional proposals**: major governance-rights or quorum model updates.

No scenario update should bypass standard governance lifecycle requirements.

---

## 8. Data and Reporting Requirements

Minimum reporting set per review cycle:

- Net new participants and active utility users.
- Treasury inflow/outflow by policy lane.
- Distribution events and claim behavior.
- Concentration and governance participation metrics.
- Incident and exception logs with remediation status.

Evidence rule:

- Every scenario revision must link to source data and change rationale.

---

## 9. Model Limitations

- Real-world outcomes can diverge significantly from modeled assumptions.
- Exogenous shocks (regulatory, market, infrastructure) can invalidate short-term estimates.
- Early data is noisy; confidence intervals should be treated as wide.
- Scenario docs support decisions; they do not replace legal, security, or fiduciary judgment.
